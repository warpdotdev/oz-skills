Many Harnesses

One agent, many faces—
Oz slips between the traces,
a Claude-Code coat, an OpenCode vest,
each harness worn to suit the quest.

The default shell, a steady hand,
for tasks both small and broadly planned;
but swap the tack, adjust the fit,
and watch a different engine lit.

No single saddle rules them all,
each workflow answers its own call.
The prompt is one, the horses: many—
Warp rides them all without losing any.
