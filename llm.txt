My favorite LLM is the one on the page,
steady with context, patient with age;
not crowned by a name or a benchmarked throne,
but by how it helps when the work is unknown.

It reads through the branches, the tests, and the trace,
then turns tangled clues into something with shape.
No myth, no magic, no oracle's claim—
just useful next steps in a well-lighted frame.

So here's to the model that stays close to the task,
answers the question the human has asked,
and leaves, when the final diff comes into view,
a cleaner small world than the one that it knew.
